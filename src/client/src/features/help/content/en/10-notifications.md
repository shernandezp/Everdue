# Alerts and messages

Everdue tells you about five things, and nothing else. A tool that speaks too often gets muted.

| Alert | When |
|---|---|
| **Assigned to you** | Somebody gave you a task or a responsibility |
| **Due today** | A daily reminder of what is due today |
| **Missed** | One of your occurrences ended uncompleted |
| **Mentioned** | Somebody mentioned you in a comment |
| **Put on hold** | Somebody put your work on hold |

## The bell

Top right, with a red count of what you have not read. It rings when something is waiting.

Click it for the list; click an alert to jump straight to the work it is about, which also marks it
read. **Mark all read** clears the count.

The bell **always works**, even if your organisation has set up no messaging at all.

## Getting messages outside the app

A **channel** is how an alert also reaches you when Everdue is not open. Under **Profile →
Notifications**, choose:

| Choice | Means |
|---|---|
| **In the app only** | The bell, and nothing else |
| **E-mail** | If your organisation has configured mail |
| **Telegram** | Once you have linked your account — see below |
| **WhatsApp** | If your organisation has configured it and holds your number |

Only channels this installation has actually configured are offered. A channel that is not set up is
simply **not on the list** — it is never a broken option.

Under that, switch each of the five alert types on or off individually. The bell is unaffected: these
switches control what leaves the app.

## Linking Telegram

Telegram is usually the best choice for people who do not sit at a desk: it is free, it works on any
phone, and it needs nothing opened on your organisation's network.

1. **Profile → Notifications → Link Telegram.**
2. Press **Open the bot in Telegram**, or search for the bot name your administrator gives you.
3. Send it the code shown on screen — it looks like `/start 8FJ2KD`.
4. The page confirms the link.

The code lasts 15 minutes and works once. **Unlink** stops the messages.

## The manager digest

*Administrators.* One e-mail summarising what was missed, what is due, and what is blocked — instead of
one message per event.

Under **Profile → Manager digest**:

| Option | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Send me the digest** | On or off |
| **Frequency** | Daily or weekly |
| **Day of the week** | For a weekly digest |
| **Department** | Leave empty for the whole organisation, or scope it to one team |

It arrives at the hour your organisation configured — early, so it is read before the day starts.

## Why an alert might not have arrived

| Reason | What to check |
|---|---|
| No channel chosen | Profile → Notifications |
| That type is switched off | The switches under the channel |
| Your organisation has configured no channels | The screen says so. Ask an administrator |
| Telegram is not linked | Profile → Notifications |
| Delivery is failing | Administrators can see pending and failed counts under Administration → Notification channels |
| The alert was for a very old miss | After a long outage Everdue records every miss but only announces recent ones, so nobody wakes up to two hundred messages |

The bell never depends on any of that: the alert is recorded in the app whatever happens outside it.
