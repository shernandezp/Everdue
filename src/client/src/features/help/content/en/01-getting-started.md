# Getting started

Welcome to Everdue. This page takes about five minutes and gets you from "I just signed in" to
"I finished something".

## What Everdue is for

Everdue answers one question: **what work needs to happen, what happened, and what needs attention?**

It is not a project manager and not a to-do app. It is for the work that comes back: the weekly call
with a client, the monthly inspection of a machine, the daily cash count. Work that never really
finishes — it just comes due again.

## Signing in

1. Open the address your organisation gave you.
2. Type your e-mail and password, then press **Sign in**.
3. If this is your first time, Everdue asks you to choose a new password. It needs at least
   **10 characters**, with a capital letter, a small letter and a digit.

> If your organisation uses Google accounts, you will also see a **Sign in with Google** button. It
> only works for an account an administrator already created for you — Everdue never creates accounts
> on its own.

Forgot your password? There is no self-service reset by design. Ask an administrator; they can set a
new one for you in seconds, and you will be asked to change it at your next sign-in.

## The screen, in one look

| Where | What is there |
|---|---|
| Left panel | Every screen you can open, grouped. Click a group's title to open or close it |
| Top left | The Everdue mark and your organisation's name |
| Top right | The bell (your alerts), **Help**, and your name |
| Under your name | Your profile, the light/dark switch, and **Sign out** |

The left panel only shows what your account may open. If you are a **Member** you see *Work*; if you
are an **Administrator** you also see *Reports*, *Insights* and *Administration*.

## Your first five minutes

**1. Open "My work".** This is your board. Each card is one piece of work, and each column is a state:

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| To do | Waiting for you |
| In progress | You have started it |
| On hold | Parked, waiting on somebody or something |
| Missed | The period ended without it being completed |
| Done | Finished in the last 7 days |

**2. Complete something.** Drag a card from **To do** to **Done**. You can also click the **⋮** menu on
the card and choose **Complete** — on a phone that is easier than dragging.

**3. Look at what happened.** Click the card you just completed. A panel slides in from the right with
its full history: who created it, who completed it, and when. Nothing is ever silently changed in
Everdue; every change is on that list with a name against it.

**4. Add a one-off task.** Press **New task**, give it a title, pick who owns it and when it is due,
and press **Create**. Use this for work that happens once. Work that repeats is a *responsibility*, and
an administrator sets those up — see [How Everdue thinks](02-key-ideas).

**5. Say something.** Open any item, scroll to **Comments**, and write what happened. If you need a
colleague to see it, use **Mention** to pick them: they get an alert with a link straight to the item.

## A promise worth knowing about

**Everdue never erases a miss.** If a period ends without the work being done, that is recorded — and
completing it late records that too, without removing the miss. That is not strictness for its own
sake: it is the only way a report months from now can tell you the truth about what actually happened.
